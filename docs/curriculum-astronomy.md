# Astronomy & Lunar Studies Curriculum — Year One

> Companion to the Herbology and Crystals docs. Blends real naked-eye astronomy
> (phases, planets, the ecliptic, Polaris, constellations, eclipses, the solar
> year) with the witch-lore that hangs off it — treated as "the common
> tradition," not scientific claims. Prof. Vega teaches one topic per class;
> each adds a Book of Shadows page and a skill point.

## How it fits the game

- **Astronomy class** (Observatory, Prof. Vega — taught Tuesdays): each lesson
  introduces one topic of sky-lore. Attending records a **Book of Shadows**
  entry (what's in the sky + how the craft uses it) and a skill point.
- It's **progressive**, like Herbology / Crystals / Magical Creatures — come
  back each week to chart the next topic until the syllabus is complete.
- Complements the live **lunar system**: the moon's phase drives gathering
  potency and the Observatory's nightly "chart the moon" action.

## Year-One syllabus

| # | Topic | In the sky | In the craft |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Eight Phases** | The Moon rounds new→full→new ~29.5 days, lit from the side by the Sun. | Wax to begin/draw in, wane to release/banish; charge water & tools at the Full Moon. |
| 2 | **The Seven Wanderers** | Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn — the classical "planets." | Each rules a weekday & virtue; time a working to its planet. |
| 3 | **The Wheel of the Zodiac** | The Sun's yearly path (ecliptic) through twelve signs. | Four elements × three signs; read the Moon's sign for the day's mood. |
| 4 | **Polaris, the Lodestar** | The North Star sits almost over the pole; the sky wheels around it. | Find north by the Plough's two pointer stars — never be lost at night. |
| 5 | **Reading the Constellations** | Great Bear, Orion, Cassiopeia — storied star-patterns. | A seasonal calendar: Orion = winter, the Summer Triangle = summer. |
| 6 | **Eclipses & Omens** | Solar at the New Moon; lunar (the "blood moon") at the Full, in Earth's shadow. | Potent thresholds — many witches rest & observe rather than cast. |
| 7 | **The Wheel of the Year** | Solstices, equinoxes, and the cross-quarter days between. | Eight sabbats mark the turning solar year. |
| 8 | **Comets & Falling Stars** | Meteors (dust burning up) and comets (icy visitors with tails). | Wish on a falling star — a folk charm for fixing an intention. |

## Notes on sources

The astronomy is standard naked-eye fact (lunar synodic period ~29.5 days, the
seven classical planets and their weekday rulerships, the twelve-sign ecliptic,
Polaris and the circumpolar stars, solstices/equinoxes and cross-quarter days,
meteors vs. comets). The magical correspondences (planetary days, elemental
zodiac, full-moon charging, wishing on stars) follow the same widely-attested
folk tradition used elsewhere in the game's curriculum docs.
