# Bestiary — Magical Creatures (mine-dwellers)

> Taught one at a time by **Keeper Fenn** in the Magical Creatures class
> (Tuesdays). Studying a creature adds a Book of Shadows page and reveals how to
> settle it in the mines. **There is no combat** — you Soothe, Frost, Ward, Offer
> food, Light a candle, or Back away. The right response also leaves a small gift.
> The spirits are grounded in real mining folklore (sources below).
>
> **Entry gate:** the mines stay shut until you've studied at least **3** creatures
> *and* own a **flute** (the rats won't part otherwise) — Keeper Fenn turns you back
> at the entrance until then.

## Mundane (surface mine — depth 1)

| Creature | Settle by | Leaves | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cave Spider** | Ward | Spider Silk | big but timid |
| **Cave Rat** | **Play a flute** | shows you ore | charmed by a tune, like the Pied Piper's rats |
| **Wasp Swarm** | Frost | Beeswax | all heat and motion |

## Spirits (deeper — depths 2–3)

| Creature | Settle by | Leaves | Folklore |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Knocker** | Offer food | leads to ore | Cornish mine-spirit; knocks to warn of cave-ins and leads the worthy to rich veins. Miners left them the last bite of a pasty; never whistle near them. |
| **Will-o'-the-Wisp** | Ward | — | A deceiving light (*ignis fatuus*) that leads travellers astray; don't follow. |
| **Kobold** | Soothe | a fine stone | German mine-spirit; helps if treated kindly, sours the ore if slighted — **cobalt** is named for it. |
| **Cave Sprite** | Soothe | — | mischievous, flighty fae. |
| **Shadow-Moths** | Light a candle | — | swarm in the dark, scatter toward light. |

## Design notes

- **Depth** raises encounter odds (+10%/level) and the chance of rarer stones.
- The **Offer** and **Light** responses consume a **fruit** / **candle** — giving the
  Fruit Stall and the candles in Magical Tools a real use.
- Studying a creature in class highlights its best response during an encounter.
- Future: a Witch's Bottle / **defense** could make Ward auto-succeed; gifts could feed
  crafting (silk for charm-bindings, wax for candles).
