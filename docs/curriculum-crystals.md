# Crystals & Lithomancy Curriculum — Year One

> Companion to the Herbology doc. Correspondences reflect widely-attested
> crystal-lore (chakra / element / planet / properties), corroborated across
> multiple sources — treated as "the common tradition," not scientific claims.
> Prof. Beryl teaches one stone per class; each adds a Book of Shadows page and
> a sample stone to your bag. Sources at the bottom.

## How it fits the game

- **Crystals class** (Academy, Prof. Beryl): each lesson introduces one stone —
  mineral, element/planet/chakra, properties, and practical use. Attending records
  a **Book of Shadows** entry, a skill point, and gives you **one sample stone**.
- **Selling:** stones are sellable to the Village merchant (each has its own value).
- **Mining (in game):** buy a **pickaxe** at the Magical Tools shop, then work ore
  seams in **The Mines** (off the Village Square). Each strike yields a random
  stone — commoner stones are likelier, and a higher **Mining** skill improves the
  odds of rarer ones. Seams deplete and replenish over days. Sell stones in the
  village. *Future:* deeper levels and an optional combat/encounter layer.

## Grounded details worth keeping

- **Amethyst fades in sunlight** — cleanse/charge it in moonlight, not sun. (Real.)
- **Most "citrine" sold is heat-treated amethyst**; natural citrine is uncommon. (Real.)
- **Moonstone** is charged under the full moon — ties neatly to our lunar system.
- **Clear quartz** is the "master healer" / amplifier, used to boost other workings —
  a natural future mechanic (a quartz in a spell boosts its effect).
